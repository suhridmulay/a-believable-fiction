This week we have visitors in office, and therefore long hours usually follow, from meetings and as a blend and consequence of traffic and other policies. I have discovered that I find this late stay enjoyable in a different sense. When one works late, there is little obligation to work from home, unfortunately there's little free time in which one does free time things as well. I am not sure what I like better. The blurring of work/home boundaries that working from home brings, is not something I fully am comfortable with.

The weather has been under a cozy cloud cover this week, this culminated in a drizzly Friday, I really enjoyed roaming around near the office in the drizzle.

![Rains Near Office](/assets/images/rain-near-office.jpg)
