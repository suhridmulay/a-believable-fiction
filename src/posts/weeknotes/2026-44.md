## General Updates

I think firstly some self congratulations are in order, I have successfully concluded (the definition of successfully being a bit stretched here) one year of maintaining this blog. Although this has been done with variable frequencies and random posts, rarely with rhyme or reason. It is probably the longest time I have spent on a single project, and I am really happy for it. In this one year I have grown more as a person than I would have imagined. I have had people bump in and out of my life - well mostly out since I am not particularly good at making friends. However I have enjoyed this exercise immensely. I think I cannot thank enough the kind people of [IndieWebClub Bangalore](https://blr.indiewebclub.org).

When I started out I had technically immodest ambitions for this website. I wanted to take a chance at discovering my old knack for frontend development and animation. However I have since found development a bit tedious and realised my intrinsic motivation is currently in short supply, so my ambitions have scaled down. I use this website more as a diary these days than anything else, but I find writing here immensely enjoyable. I think in the next year, _I would like myself to be more ambitious with my website, and give shape to ideas I have otherwise been reluctant to try_.

I also bought a subscription to Mistral AI to play with LLMs and experiment. I have (like every other engineer) been using them for work, however I think I would now like to explore agents independently as well. I have some cool projects in my mind I would like to try.

Diwali is here, which means cleaning time. Oddly enough I find in me an appreciation for chores now. Who knew the tedium of domesticity would have a certain allure to it? It is certainly tiring, but the tiredness turns cleanliness into its own reward. If anything I guess that such is the nature of life, you need the less fancy parts to make the fancy parts glow.

With Diwali also comes spending, since I am still working I also have working money, that is to a euphemism for purchasing power. I have affected two big purchases. One is a pre order for possibly the biggest gaming phenomenon of time, that is Grand Theft Auto VI, and the other is a keyboard and mouse pair since my last pair is now showing its age. All of this has racked up into a rather impressive bill, especially with my Goa Trip, of which I shall write in a followup blog. I must be more fiscally prudent.

And on that note, I must now conclude. Surely there is much and more to the week and my memory fails me on several counts, but I guess it is for me to keep private.
