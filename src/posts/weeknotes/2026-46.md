## Week 45

Happy Diwali to all who keep the festival. I have made a small greeting for you all on [a special page on this website](/greetings/diwali). Please visit, play around and let me know your thoughts.

This week (or rather the tail end of this, week) has been otherwise really happening, especially if you're involved with technology. Microsoft had a multi day outage on Friday, to the delight of IT and service workers in Asia I assume. Although things did recover by the time our peers in the United States came online. A tragedy by all accounts, more so for our American brethren. Press <kbd>F</kbd> to pay respects. Nvidia CEO also decided this would be a good time to discover his moral compass and disclose, at least one of his long standing affairs. After it was revealed that his mistress is possibly a Chinese asset.

I find myself spending a lot of time at work this week. No doubt unhealthy by most means and measures known to man. I have also been introspecting about my nature of work. It seems as one moves up the career ladder ones role transforms from being a contributor to being a go to person. I now sit on the approving end of designs, code reviews, and other such. On other times I also find myself directly interacting with customers who use our products and understanding their frustrations and channeling those into feature ideas. The simplest of which Claude can implement while the rest are shelved until such time comes. I did not want to be a product person, I am much happier firefighting and being an "engineer", but it seems - as I always say - wyrd bið ful aræd. Fate is wholly inexorable, to everyone.

I also in a fit of boredom completed my reading of one of Bernard Cromwell's Uhtred Books, this time the one where he gets captured and sold to slavery by Guhtred. A quick Google search tells me, the book is [Lords of The North](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6220255-lords-of-the-north). Every time I read a part of Uhtred's story, I am very tempted to play Assassin's Creed Valhalla, but Ubisoft's mediocrity and disservice to the time period puts me off every time I boot the game.

On a side note, my reading of the Saxon Stories is partly the reason behind the [first post on this blog](/posts/longforms/england-i). I find the history of England to be very exciting. If you're looking for a book recommendation, I would really advise you to read any of Saxon Stories, or Warlord Chronicles to develop an interest in the topic.

## Week 46

We are off to a flying start on Monday, just few more days to go to until the biggest media event of all time, the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI. I have pre-ordered the game, certainly not a fiscally sound choice, although I have not been doing much of that so it is well within the grand scheme of things. I have also picked up a new book - which I haven't gotten a moment's peace to read. [Transformer by Nick Lane](https://nick-lane.net/books/transformer-the-deep-chemistry-of-life-and-death/). Molecular biology is actually pretty interesting who knew? Ha.

Midweek was worse than usual this time around, of course a Java vulnerability had to drop in the middle of the week destroying any semblance of peace I had in the mornings, since at my day job I own a public facing Java application it's time to secure that. For a 9:00 AM determination to being secure by 11:00 AM is probably an achievement by corporate standards. I must genuinely thank the InfoSec team at my workplace.

I have been resisting very hard, the temptation to play Grand Theft Auto 6 on my Playstation. While I hear praises all around I am waiting for an empty and clear mind to begin my own exploration.
