Many people have made commentaries on there being two Indias, mostly to justify some opinion they hold. With this post I want to add mine to the mix. However instead of emphasising the difference I want to draw some attention to how or why some differences might be.

The two Indias I want to talk about are North and South India, and the difference between them, the Vindhyan mountains. Often considered a very culturally significant divide between North and South India. North of the Vindhyas is the very fertile Indo-Gangetic plain, with it's massive meltwater fed rivers the agriculture of the Indo-Gangetic plains (one of the oldest in the world) keeps India fed. South of the Vindhyas, is the Deccan plateau and it's semi arid landscape, streaked with it's many many peninsular rivers, most of them rain and groundwater fed. The deccan plateau is home to South Indian states, often the economic heart of India.
